Local Wikipedia Explorer finds Wikipedia articles associated with places near a town, landmark, or postcode. Type a location and choose a matching place, or pan and zoom the map and press Search this map area. You can also tap an empty map location, then confirm Find articles here (or cancel) to use those coordinates. Tapping an existing article marker still opens its Wikipedia link. Explore the results on an interactive map and in a distance-sorted list. The default radius is 1.5 miles; if fewer than 25 articles are found, the tool progressively expands its search to a maximum of 6 miles. You can select another starting radius. At most 500 results are displayed, and distances are straight-line rather than walking routes.

The page runs in your browser but requires internet access. Search text is sent to the public Photon geocoding service, selected coordinates, including map-picked points, are sent to Wikipedia, and map tiles are loaded from OpenStreetMap. No browser geolocation is requested, and the tool does not put your location in the page URL. External providers can observe requests. Wikipedia geocoding coverage is incomplete, and its API limits can make dense-area results incomplete.

If an API request fails, try again later or search a more specific place name. The map uses Leaflet 1.9.4 loaded from a version-pinned CDN. Article titles and locations are supplied by Wikipedia contributors; map data is from OpenStreetMap contributors.
