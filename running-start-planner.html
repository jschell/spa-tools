Running Start Planner

Track Riverview School District (Cedarcrest) graduation requirements and the Bellevue College Associate in Arts & Sciences (DTA) together, and find courses that count toward both.

Advising aid only. College courses map to high school credit using the Riverview Running Start equivalency sheet (5 college credits = 1.0 HS credit) and the BC 2025–26 AA-DTA worksheet. The HS credit totals below are Washington's 24-credit framework defaults, so edit them to match your class year. Check your plan with a Cedarcrest counselor and a BC advisor. Your data stays in this browser and in the share link.

1. Your courses

Add Running Start/college courses you've taken, are taking, or plan to take. Enter codes like ENGL& 101, HIST 146, or BIOL 100 5 (credits default to 5). Separate several with commas or new lines. Classes taken at the high school go in the "Earned at HS" column in section 2.

3. Bellevue College AA-DTA

Rules applied: each course counts in only one area. Humanities, Social Sciences and Natural Sciences each need two different subjects. Humanities allows at most 5 performance/skills (*) credits and 5 credits of one 100-level language. Natural Sciences needs a lab and allows at most 5 MATH/PHIL 120 credits. Electives allow at most 3 PE activity credits and 15 restricted credits. You also need one Diversity course and a 2.0 GPA, and at least 30 of the 90 credits must be taken at BC. Cross-listed courses (e.g. PHIL 201 / POLS 201) count only once, so don't enter both.

    5. Build a plan

    Auto-fill adds the highest-value courses one at a time and places each in the earliest quarter that has room and meets its prerequisites, until both credentials are complete or the quarters are full. Running Start usually covers up to 15 college credits per quarter in Fall, Winter and Spring, and doesn't cover summer.
