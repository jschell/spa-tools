Running Start Planner

Track Riverview School District (Cedarcrest) graduation requirements and the Bellevue College Associate in Arts & Sciences (DTA) together, and find courses that count toward both.

Advising aid only. College courses map to high school credit using the Riverview Running Start equivalency sheet (5 college credits = 1.0 HS credit) and the BC 2025–26 AA-DTA worksheet. HS requirements come from the Cedarcrest 2026–27 course catalog (Classes of 2027–2030, 24 credits) and the OSPI Class of 2027 graduation toolkit. The totals are editable in case your class year differs. Check your plan with a Cedarcrest counselor and a BC advisor. Your data stays in this browser and in the share link.

Cedarcrest HS graduation (Classes of 2027–2030)

Non-credit requirements

Graduation Pathway (an ELA option and a math option):

Dual Credit pathway (OSPI Class of 2027 toolkit): earning at least 1.0 HS credit through Running Start in a 100-level or higher course that the high school credits as English meets the ELA part, and the same for math. A 5-credit ENGL 101 and a 5-credit MATH 1xx together meet the whole pathway.

Equivalencies: English = any ENGL 101+; US History = HIST 146/147/148/150; Civics = POLS 202; World History = HIST 101/102/103/128; WA History = HIST 214; Math = any MATH (below 100 counts but isn't paid by Running Start); Science = ASTR, BIOL, BOTAN, CHEM, ENVS, GEOL, METR, NSCI, NUTR, OCEA, PHYS; World Language = ARAB, ASL, CHIN, FRCH, GERM, ITAL, JAPN, SPAN; Health = HLTH 250; PE = PE, DANCE, NUTR; Art = ART, DANCE, DRMA, MUSC; CTE = ACCT, AHE*, ASL, BA, BUS, BUSIT, BTS, CS, CJ, DBA, DMA, DEV, ECED, EDUC, ECON, ENGR, IMAGE, IT, INDES, MKTG, NSCOM, PROG, TECH; Electives = any 100+ course. High school classes earn 0.5 credit per semester (1.0 for a full year), so enter semesters completed. One 5-credit college quarter = 1.0 HS credit, the same as a full year at the high school. Extra credits in any area spill into electives. Social Studies totals 3.0: World History 1.0, US History 1.0, Civics 0.5 and a 0.5 social studies elective. Cedarcrest's electives are Economics, Global Issues, Law & Business Ethics, Psychology and AP Human Geography, so this tool assumes ECON, PSYC, GEOG, HIST and POLS courses can fill the elective. That's an inference, so confirm with a counselor. Science is 3.0 credits, and 2.0 must be lab science. Running Start courses count as lab only if BC marks them (L). Per the catalog, 1.0 of the 2.0 Arts credits and both World Language credits can be Personal Pathway Requirement (PPR) courses, so lower those numbers if your HSBP uses PPR.

Bellevue College AA-DTA

Rules applied: each course counts in only one area. Humanities, Social Sciences and Natural Sciences each need two different subjects. Humanities allows at most 5 performance/skills (*) credits and 5 credits of one 100-level language. Natural Sciences needs a lab and allows at most 5 MATH/PHIL 120 credits. Electives allow at most 3 PE activity credits and 15 restricted credits. You also need one Diversity course and a 2.0 GPA, and at least 30 of the 90 credits must be taken at BC. Cross-listed courses (e.g. PHIL 201 / POLS 201) count only once, so don't enter both.

    4. Build a plan

    Auto-fill adds the highest-value courses one at a time and places each in the earliest quarter that has room and meets its prerequisites, until both credentials are complete or the quarters are full. Running Start usually covers up to 15 college credits per quarter in Fall, Winter and Spring, and doesn't cover summer.
