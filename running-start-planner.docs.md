Running Start Planner helps a Riverview School District (Cedarcrest High School, Duvall WA) student in Running Start track high school graduation requirements and the Bellevue College Associate in Arts & Sciences (DTA) transfer degree at the same time. Enter the college courses you've taken or plan to take, plus the high school credits you've already earned in each subject. The tool works out where each course counts. It uses the district's Running Start equivalency sheet (5 college credits = 1.0 HS credit) and the BC 2025–26 AA-DTA worksheet rules: one area per course, two subjects per distribution, a lab science, caps on performance, language and PE credits, and the diversity requirement.

The tool assigns courses to requirement areas automatically to fill as many gaps as possible. It also lists suggested next courses ranked by how much of both credentials each one would complete, which favors courses that "double-dip", such as HIST 146 counting for US History and Social Sciences. The auto-fill button builds a quarter-by-quarter plan using a credits-per-quarter limit and simple prerequisites such as ENGL 101 before ENGL 2xx and language sequences.

The HS credit totals default to Washington's 24-credit framework and can be edited to match your class year. The tool is an advising aid, so confirm your plan with a high school counselor and a BC advisor. Your plan is saved in your browser and in the page URL, so you can share it with a link.
