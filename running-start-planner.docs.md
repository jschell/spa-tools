Running Start Planner helps a Riverview School District (Cedarcrest High School, Duvall WA) student in Running Start plan high school graduation and the Bellevue College Associate in Arts & Sciences (DTA) transfer degree at the same time. High school requirements follow the Cedarcrest 2026–27 course catalog for the Classes of 2027–2030: 24 credits, including 2.0 lab science and a 3.0 social studies split of World History, US History, Civics and an elective. College credit converts to high school credit per OSPI guidance and the Riverview equivalency sheet: 5 quarter credits = 1.0 HS credit, while a high school semester class = 0.5 credit. The tool also tracks Washington State History, financial literacy, the High School and Beyond Plan, and the OSPI Graduation Pathway. The Dual Credit pathway is detected automatically when a Running Start English and a Running Start math course each earn 1.0 HS credit.

The requirement map shows one card per requirement, HS and AA, listing every course option from the Riverview equivalency sheet and the BC worksheet. A badge on each card says whether it is Required (a specific course or list you must pick from, such as "pick 1 of 4" for US History or "POLS 202 is the only option" for Civics), Flexible (credits any course can fill, such as electives) or Met (further picks there are optional). Click a course to add it to your plan and click again to remove it. Courses marked 2× count toward both a high school subject and an AA distribution. You can filter by course code or show only those double-count courses. Each course counts once per credential, and the tool works out the best placement automatically, applying BC rules such as two subjects per distribution, a required lab, the caps on performance, language and PE credits, the Diversity course, and the College Success requirement (FYS 101, 3 credits, taken in the first or second quarter at BC).

AP is handled in two parts. An AP class counts toward the diploma as Cedarcrest semesters like any other class. An AP exam score can be added separately: the tool converts it to Bellevue College courses using BC's official AP chart (for example, a 5 on Calculus AB becomes MATH& 151 and 152). That credit counts toward the AA only, graded S, within BC's limits of 15 S-graded elective credits and at least 30 credits taken at BC. A score of 3 or higher on an OSPI-approved English or math exam also meets that part of the Graduation Pathway. List your Cedarcrest classes in the class list (name, requirement, semesters, grade, and status of Completed, In progress or Committed). The requirement cards total them automatically and show in-progress and committed credit separately, and a by-grade view mirrors the High School and Beyond Plan worksheet. The summary only says Complete once everything is actually completed, and says On track while classes are still pending. Also track Running Start courses in the course list as Planned, In progress or Completed as you go. The suggestions list ranks courses by how much of both credentials each would complete, and auto-fill builds a quarter-by-quarter plan within a credits-per-quarter limit. The tool is an advising aid, so confirm your plan with a Cedarcrest counselor and a BC advisor. Plans are saved as named profiles in this browser's local storage on your device, so one family can keep a plan per student, or try what-if copies. Create, rename, duplicate, delete and switch profiles from the bar at the top. Use Export and Import to back up a profile or move it to another device, or copy the share link to send a plan; opening someone's link adds it as a new profile instead of replacing yours. Nothing is uploaded. Each course in your list links to the Bellevue College catalog and class search so you can check which quarters it runs. The page works on phones and tablets as well as desktops. Design decisions, sources and the roadmap for quarter-by-quarter availability are recorded in running-start-planner.design.md.
