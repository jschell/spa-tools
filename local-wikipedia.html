Local Wikipedia Explorer

Find Wikipedia articles about places near a town, landmark, or postcode. Distances are straight-line, not walking routes.

Search for a place to begin.

Or tap an empty spot on the map to choose a search center.

Nearby articles

    Privacy and data: Typed searches are sent to the public Photon geocoder; selected coordinates (including points chosen on the map) are sent to Wikipedia for nearby article lookup. Map tiles are requested from OpenStreetMap. No device location is requested, and searches or coordinates are not saved in this page's URL. External services can observe requests. This tool needs an internet connection.
